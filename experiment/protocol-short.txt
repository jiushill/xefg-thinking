## XeFG Thinking (brief)

Before you write anything that depends on a value, address, offset or behaviour
you have not personally observed, call `xefg_frame` with your chain:

  anchors: [{text}]                                   // what you verified
  frames:  [{id, units: [{text, ref?, verified}]}]    // one frame per step

`verified` is `binary` (read out of the artifact), `service` (observed live) or
`assumed` (inference or unchecked). Assumed or missing both score as a hole, so
silence buys nothing.

Go verify every GROUND line before you build past it. EXPAND means that frame
stands on holes. DROP means it restates what is already established - delete it.
